--- IEC958 Playback Default (numid=15) during playback ---
  : values=[AES0=0x06 AES1=0x82 AES2=0x00 AES3=0x02]

S16_LE / 2ch / 48000 is the IEC61937 carrier, and AES0 bit 1 set is what marks it
as a compressed burst rather than PCM. Both match the low-level proof in
lowlevel-ac3-hwparams.txt exactly, which is the point: Kodi ended up driving the
hardware in precisely the configuration that was proven by hand first.

For contrast, the same sink opened for ordinary PCM shows AES0=0x04 and
aeformat=15/AE_FMT_S24NE4 at 6 or 2 channels - see kodi-audio-enumeration.txt and
the transcode lines in transcode-result.txt.
