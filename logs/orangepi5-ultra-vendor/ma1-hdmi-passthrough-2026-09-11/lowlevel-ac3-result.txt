Why this gate mattered
  A pass here fixes the fault domain before anything else is touched. The kernel,
  the dw-hdmi-qp controller, the I2S path and the sink were all proven to carry a
  real compressed burst with no patch of any kind. Everything that failed later was
  therefore above this line, and no kernel or DT work was warranted - which is what
  section 10 of the brief asks to be established before any code is written.

The one non-default step
  The card powers up with AES0=0x04, non-audio clear. Sent that way the same bytes
  are decoded by the sink as PCM and come out as noise. `iecset -c 0 audio off`
  sets the flag. This is not a workaround left in place: config/alsa/rockchip-hdmi1.conf
  now carries it as a per-stream ctl_elems hook, so Kodi's own AES0=0x06 is applied
  when a compressed stream opens and the byte is restored to 0x04 on close.
