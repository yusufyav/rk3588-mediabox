### What this means for Gate MA1

Declared compressed formats (the only ones in test scope):
  AC-3    6ch  32/44.1/48 kHz  max 640 kbps
  DTS     6ch  32/44.1/48 kHz  max 1504 kbps
  E-AC-3  8ch  44.1/48 kHz

NOT declared, therefore not enabled and not tested:
  MLP / TrueHD  (CEA format code 12) - absent
  DTS-HD        (CEA format code 11) - absent

Kodi lists TrueHD and DTS-HD for this device anyway. That is not sink evidence:
CAESinkALSA::EnumerateDevice pushes its full stream-type list onto any
AE_DEVTYPE_HDMI device with the comment "we don't trust ELD information and
push back our supported formats explicitly". The ELD above is the authority.

### Declared vs. actually reproduced

The sink over-declares. Measured at the TV, with a clean stream at every
machine-visible layer in each case:

  AC-3        declared, reproduced          PASS
  E-AC-3      declared, reproduced          PASS
  DTS         declared, SILENT at the sink  not usable
  LPCM 6ch    declared, SILENT at the sink  not usable (PCM path stays 2.0)

So ELD presence was necessary to scope the gate but was not sufficient to
decide what to leave enabled. Both DTS entries are declared by a sink that
cannot play them.
