OPEN: horizontal picture shift when the GUI plane joins the composition
=======================================================================

2026-09-10. Reported by the operator after the 0009 acceptance run, on BOTH
sinks (BenQ RD280UG and HP X27q). Unresolved at this commit. Recorded with
what was eliminated so the next session does not repeat it.

The symptom, as narrowed with the operator
------------------------------------------
Initially reported as "the film shifts right and left when I pause and start".
Two clarifications changed the shape of the problem:

  * It is NOT specific to pause. Toggling the OSD three times with the film
    playing continuously reproduced it. The trigger is the GUI plane becoming
    active, which pausing happens to do.
  * It jumps once and stays -- two stable positions, not a wobble or a
    transient.

What changes in the machine when the GUI plane joins
----------------------------------------------------
A full state capture was taken playing and with the OSD up, and diffed. The
complete set of differences:

  crtc 89          fb 0 -> fb 222
  plane 57 (GUI)   disabled -> crtc=89 fb=222
  plane 73 (video) fb 255 -> 250        (next buffer from the pool; normal)
  VOP              gains Cluster0-win0 ACTIVE, AR24, src/dst 2560x1440

Nothing else. In particular the video plane's CRTC_X/Y/W/H and SRC_X/Y/W/H are
byte-identical in both states.

Eliminated, each by direct measurement
--------------------------------------
  video plane geometry   full DRM property dump, both states: identical
                         (dst pos[0,28] rect[2560x1384] throughout)
  buffer stride          pitch 4864 across 25 samples, no variation
  composition path       overlay_mode[1] in both states -- it does not fall
                         back to 0 (that was the 0008-only behaviour, and is
                         what 0009 fixes)
  renderer switch        Kodi logs one CRenderManager::Configure, at playback
                         start; none on pause, resume or OSD toggle
  a GL copy of the video Kodi's own screenshot with the GUI plane active is
                         entirely black -- the GUI plane carries no video
  HDMI re-training       dmesg cleared, four OSD toggles: zero kernel events.
                         No modeset, no link re-train, no underflow
  VOP2 registers         active_regs diffed. Within the Esmart0 (video) block
                         the only change is the two buffer addresses; the
                         control word 00010029 and the stride word 04c004c0
                         (= 1216*4 = 4864, matching the measured pitch) are
                         unchanged. The changes are confined to the overlay
                         mixer and the newly added Cluster0 block

So on the RK3588 side, down to the register level, the video window is
programmed identically in both states. The signal the SoC emits does not move
the picture horizontally.

Measurement attempts that failed, and why
-----------------------------------------
Two rounds of operator photography could not settle it:

  * Two handheld stills: camera angle and film scene both changed between
    them, so the apparent shift and size change are not separable from
    perspective.
  * A 16 s handheld video. Frames were extracted and the logo position was
    measured normalised to the panel's own edges, to cancel camera motion.
    The normalised centre ranges 0.545..0.571 with no discrete step -- camera
    movement is larger than the signal being looked for.

This is why the operator's observation and the machine evidence are both
recorded here rather than one being used to overrule the other.

What remains
------------
Two hypotheses survive, and they need different instruments:

  1. The sink rescales. Both panels would have to do it, and no HDMI re-train
     is visible from the kernel, which weakens it -- but a panel can re-apply
     its own scaler without anything reaching the SoC. Worth checking whether
     the monitor's on-screen info popup appears at the moment of the shift,
     and what its aspect/scaling mode is set to.

  2. VOP2 composition shifts the video when a Cluster window is enabled.
     This would be invisible to every software-visible property, which is
     consistent with everything above.

The instrument that separates them is the Writeback connector, which this SoC
exposes (`Writeback-1`) and which captures the composited VOP output digitally.
If a writeback capture shows the shift, it is hypothesis 2; if it is clean, it
is hypothesis 1. Kodi holds DRM master, so this needs a small dedicated probe
rather than an ad-hoc command.

A cheaper first step is a controlled photograph: camera braced and untouched,
same film frame, one exposure with the OSD up and one without. That is enough
to align on the bezel and difference the two.

Relation to patches 0008/0009
-----------------------------
Not established either way. 0009 changes what happens when the GUI plane joins
during HDR playback -- it keeps overlay_mode at 1 where 0008 alone let it fall
to 0 -- so it is a candidate, and the A/B is set up but was not completed: the
0008-only binary is kept on the appliance as
`/opt/rk3588-mediabox/kodi/lib/kodi/kodi-gbm.pre0009`, alongside the current
one as `kodi-gbm.with0009`, so the comparison can be run directly.

What is established is that 0009 fixes a real and separately confirmed defect
(the washed player controls), and that the shift is reported on both sinks.
