S0 / S1 / S2 / S3 - the colour chain across one Kodi session
============================================================

Sink: BenQ RD280UG. NOTE: NOT the Sony BRAVIA KD-65XE9005 that Gates MP1a and
MP1b were judged against. Its native mode is 3840x2560p50 (3:2), which is what
made the mode-selection problem below visible at all. Capability and operator
observations: tv-state.txt.

Readings
--------
1. S3 is identical to S0 in every field. The whole HDR acquisition is released
   on stop -- metadata cleared, depth back to Automatic, colorimetry back to
   Default, the link back to RGB, and the mode restored.

   Getting S3 right needed one more fix than expected. Kodi calls
   SetColorimetry(nullptr) from the renderer's destructor, which runs BEFORE
   the video plane is released, so a decision based on the plane still saw YUV
   and left the GUI on a YCbCr link. A null picture means "the GUI is what will
   be on screen"; that is now what the decision uses.

2. The SDR GUI carries no colour conversion at all: csc mode[0], r2y[0], full
   range RGB on the wire. There is no matrix and no quantisation range for the
   sink to get wrong, which is what fixed the washed-out GUI the operator
   reported.

3. S1 reproduces the Gate MP1b baseline on a different sink: NV15 on the
   Esmart video plane, BT.2020 Limited, csc mode[3], 10-bit YCbCr 4:2:2 on the
   wire, HDR10 signalled, 3840x2160 at 23.976.

4. S2 keeps every bit of it with the OSD on screen -- same bus format, same 10
   bits, same HDR10 tag, same BT2020_YCC, same csc mode[3] on the video
   window. Three open/close cycles, all identical (playback-stats.txt).

5. color_format reads ycbcr444 in S1/S2 while the wire is 4:2:2. That is the
   driver negotiating the requested format down to fit 4K24 at 10 bits, and it
   is why bus_format is the authority in this gate and the property readback
   is not.

6. The video path is deliberately NOT RGB. Requesting RGB there was measured:
   the link comes up RGB888_1X24, which is 8-bit, while ycbcr444 + 30bit gives
   a real 10-bit YUYV10_1X20. Trading HDR10 down to 8 bits to win a range
   argument is the wrong trade.

Separately, and not a colour change: before the mode whitelist was populated,
playback stayed at the desktop's 3840x2560p50 and never reached 23.976. Kodi's
default whitelist admits only modes at least as large as the current one, and
on a 3:2 native panel 3840x2160 is shorter than the desktop, so every film
mode was excluded. With the whitelist filled from Kodi's own option list the
log reads "[WHITELIST] Matched an exact resolution with an exact refresh rate
3840x2160 @ 23.976000 Hz".
