Kodi GUI under llvmpipe and under Mali-G610
date: 2026-09-10T11:04:42+03:00

Same Kodi binary, same patch set, same appliance profile, same scripted
navigation over JSON-RPC, same GUI resolution (the sink's native
3840x2560). The only difference between the two runs is MEDIABOX_GPU,
which is the only difference in LD_LIBRARY_PATH. Nothing is installed
for either; the system Mesa is untouched throughout.

The CPU and memory figures barely move, which is the point: llvmpipe was
not saturating the CPU, it was simply unable to fill a 3840x2560 frame in
anything like a frame's time. At 1.6 fps the GUI is not slow, it is
unusable. Operator's assessment of the Mali run, unprompted: "belirgin
sekilde akici" -- clearly smooth.

The buffer figures are the other half of the story. Under Mali the GUI
holds 5 dmabufs and 236 MB of display IOVA at 3840x2560 -- five buffers
of 39.3 MB, exactly the bound patches/kodi/0003 imposes. Before that
patch this same GUI consumed the entire 4 GiB arena in about eight
seconds and Kodi died; see gbm-buffer-leak.txt.
