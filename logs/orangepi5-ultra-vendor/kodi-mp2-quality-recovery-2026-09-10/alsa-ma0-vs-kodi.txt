Gate MA0's working probe params against Kodi's, side by side
============================================================

Both drive the same endpoint: card 0, rockchip-hdmi1 i2s-hifi-0, the HDMI
playback PCM. MA0 opened it as hw:0,0; Kodi opens sysdefault:CARD=rockchiphdmi1,
which is the same card through ALSA's sysdefault plugin -- snd_pcm_name()
confirms the device Kodi actually got.

  parameter          MA0 probe (proven)        Kodi 22 AESinkALSA
  -----------------  ------------------------  ---------------------------
  device             hw:0,0                    sysdefault:CARD=rockchiphdmi1
  access             RW_INTERLEAVED            RW_INTERLEAVED
  format             S16_LE                    S24_LE
  rate               48000                     48000
  channels           2                         2
  period_size        256                       2400
  buffer_size        131072                    9600
  buffer in time     ~2.73 s                   0.200 s
  period in time     ~5.3 ms                   50 ms
  start_threshold    (probe default)           2147483647 (start on demand)
  avail_min          (probe default)           2400 (= one period)
  chmap              not requested             FL FR, accepted (ret=0)
  xruns              0                         0

Readings
--------
1. The formats differ and both are correct. MA0 swept the formats and found
   S16_LE, S24_LE and S32_LE all granted; Kodi asks for float, is refused, and
   settles on S24NE4 -> S24_LE. Nothing is being resampled or substituted in
   either case.

2. The buffer sizes differ by a factor of thirteen, and Kodi's is the one this
   gate wanted. MA0's 131072 frames is the driver's default when nothing asks
   for anything else -- 2.7 s of latency, which MA0 itself flagged as
   unacceptable to inherit. Kodi asks for 2400/9600 and gets exactly that:
   200 ms of buffer, 50 ms periods. The requirement that Kodi must not fall
   back to the uncontrolled default is met.

3. Neither side has an initialisation problem. Kodi's init is healthy in every
   field, and its first snd_pcm_writei returns all 2400 frames with the PCM
   still PREPARED. The -77 that stopped Gate MP2 happens later and has nothing
   to do with these parameters -- see alsa-init-trace.txt.

4. What MA0 could not have caught: MA0 sets its display mode once, before
   playback, and never again. Kodi switches the mode mid-stream to reach the
   film's 23.976 Hz, and that is what stops the PCM. A probe that never
   re-trains the HDMI link cannot see this failure, which is why MA0 passed
   cleanly and Kodi did not.
