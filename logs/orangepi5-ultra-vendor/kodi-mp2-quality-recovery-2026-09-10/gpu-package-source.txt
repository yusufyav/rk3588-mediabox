Variant choice: the plain -gbm asset. The release also ships -x11-gbm,
-wayland-gbm, -x11-wayland-gbm and -dummy variants. Kodi runs standalone
GBM/DRM here with no X11 and no Wayland compositor, so the GBM-only
variant is the one that matches the runtime and pulls the fewest
libraries. -dummy is a no-hardware stub and is not a candidate.
