Residual finding: 0008 does not restore the video plane's EOTF
--------------------------------------------------------------
At S0 and S3 the video plane reads EOTF=2 while disabled (crtc=0, fb=0). It
was first noticed with Kodi not running at all, and the log above shows why:
`CVideoLayerBridgeDRMPRIME::Disable()` is called on stop and logs, but unlike
0009's GUI path it never writes EOTF back to 0. The property stays latched at
whatever the last played file set.

No visual impact was observed and none is expected while the plane is
disabled, and Kodi itself is self-consistent because 0008 rewrites EOTF from
the picture on every Configure(). The exposure is a different DRM client --
the MP1b raw probe, or Kodi replaying SDR content through a path that does not
set the property -- inheriting a stale PQ tag. It is also an asymmetry with
0009, which does restore SDR on teardown, and with this gate's own stated
acceptance criterion that stop should return the plane to EOTF=0.

Recorded as open. `Disable()` is the place for the fix.
